Case Study

From 9K Followers to a Structured Lead-Generation System

How ARKVUE helped a trading education business turn existing Instagram attention into a clearer, more active lead-generation engine.

Case Study 01

Ujjwal Dutta (trade.with.ud)
Trading Education & Mentorship

Turning an existing audience into a structured lead-generation system.

The Challenge

Ujjwal had built an Instagram audience of nearly 9,000 followers, but his content wasn’t consistently converting that attention into enquiries.

The conversion journey had several gaps:

  • Content was generating attention but wasn’t consistently engineered around conversion.
  • There was no active Instagram → Telegram acquisition flow.
  • Telegram onboarding had stalled.
  • There was no structured nurturing system inside the community.
  • Leads weren’t being sufficiently pre-qualified before entering sales conversations.

The problem wasn’t attention. It was converting existing attention into a structured customer journey.

What ARKVUE Built — 30-Day Growth Sprint

01 — Instagram Conversion System

We rebuilt the profile positioning, CTA architecture and content structure so that attention had a clear next step toward Telegram or the relevant offer.

02 — Telegram Conversion System

We rebuilt the customer journey:

Instagram → Telegram → Onboarding → Nurturing → Buyer Intent

This included restructuring the Telegram onboarding experience and introducing a more active nurturing system inside the community.

03 — Conversion-Led Content

We shifted the content strategy toward attracting relevant prospects, signalling buying intent and moving them toward the next step — not simply generating views.

04 — Sales Activation

We connected the nurtured Telegram audience to live Zoom calls, creating a direct path from community engagement to sales conversations.

The Result — 30 Days

~200 Telegram members (up from ~50)
10–15/day Instagram enquiries during the campaign
~4× Community growth in 30 days

The Transformation

Before ARKVUE

9K Instagram followers → Content → Low conversion → No active Telegram flow → Limited nurturing → ~1–2 enquiries/day

After ARKVUE

Instagram audience → Conversion-led content → Structured CTA → Telegram → Onboarding + nurturing → Sales activation → 10–15 enquiries/day during the campaign

The Outcome

In 30 days, ARKVUE helped transform a fragmented customer journey into a more structured:

Instagram → Telegram → Nurturing → Sales

system — turning existing audience attention into a clearer and more active lead-generation engine.
