Who We Help

Brokerages

Acquire more qualified traders. Build a better activation engine. Generating traffic is only the beginning — ARKVUE helps founder-led trading brokerages improve the systems that acquire, qualify and activate higher-intent traders.

The Problem

More leads don't necessarily mean more active traders.

Brokerages can generate significant traffic and registrations while still struggling to turn that demand into qualified, activated traders.

Common gaps include:

  • High volumes of low-quality leads.
  • Weak qualification before registration.
  • Poor visibility into lead sources and quality.
  • Drop-off between enquiry and registration, and between registration and activation.
  • Fragmented acquisition channels and manual follow-up processes.

Traffic exists. Registrations may exist. But the system connecting demand → qualified trader → activation isn't working efficiently.

ARKVUE Approach

Build the system around your growth bottleneck.

A brokerage doesn't necessarily need another acquisition channel — it may have a qualification, conversion or activation problem instead. ARKVUE diagnoses before it prescribes.

Typical focus areas

  • Acquisition — Positioning, content and conversion systems, landing pages, lead capture, campaign architecture and channel optimization.
  • Qualification — Lead scoring, intent signals, application flows, lead routing and qualification criteria that separate serious traders from casual leads.
  • Conversion — Enquiry-to-registration journeys, follow-up systems, sales infrastructure and WhatsApp or other communication flows.
  • Activation — Registration-to-activation journeys, onboarding, trader communication and engagement systems.
  • Retention — Re-engagement, community systems, feedback loops and ongoing retention touchpoints.

Trader growth journey: Demand → Lead → Qualified Trader → Registration → Activation → Active Trader → Retention
